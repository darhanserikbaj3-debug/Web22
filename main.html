Essential Cooking Secrets

Master key techniques used by professional chefs to elevate simple ingredients into perfect dishes.

01

Water Ratio

Follow the golden 1-10-100 rule: 1 liter of water and 10 grams of salt for every 100 grams of pasta.

Learn More
02

Al Dente Precision

Boil the pasta for exactly 1 minute less than indicated on the package to finish cooking in the sauce.

Learn More
03

Save Pasta Water

Reserve half a cup of starchy pasta water to create a smooth, creamy sauce emulsion without heavy cream.
